# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository hosts public comments for articles published on a-nomad.com. We want discussions to remain useful, welcoming, and safe for readers with different backgrounds and opinions.

Participants are expected to:

- Discuss the article or a clearly related question in good faith.
- Be respectful and patient, including when correcting or disagreeing with someone.
- Share relevant experience, evidence, and references without presenting speculation as fact.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove sensitive information if it is posted by mistake.
- Follow GitHub's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, hate speech, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information, sexual or violent content directed at people, impersonation, spam, repeated self-promotion, illegal content, and attempts to disrupt the discussion.

## Moderation

The repository maintainers may edit the discussion state, hide or delete comments, lock discussions, limit interaction, or restrict accounts when necessary to protect participants and keep the forum useful. Moderation may be applied to content or behavior that is abusive, irrelevant, promotional, illegal, sensitive, or otherwise inconsistent with this policy.

A moderation action is not a forum for public argument. If you believe an action was made in error, use the private contact channel below and include the discussion URL and a short explanation.

## Sensitive or private matters

Do not use public Discussions for account issues, security reports, copyright disputes, payment or order information, or private feedback. Use the private contact channel on [a-nomad.com](https://a-nomad.com/contact/) instead.

## Scope

This policy applies to Discussions, comments, replies, issue reports, pull requests, and other public activity in this repository. It also applies when a participant represents the repository in another public space.

## Contact

For moderation questions or sensitive matters, contact the site owner privately through [a-nomad.com/contact/](https://a-nomad.com/contact/). Do not include secrets or credentials in the report.
